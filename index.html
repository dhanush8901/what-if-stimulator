What-If Business Simulator

Problem

Management wants +20% sales but does not know if employees, inventory, production and logistics can support it. Simulate consequences of strategic decisions.

Algorithm flow

InputProcessDecisionOutput
Current baseline: sales, headcount, stock, production & logistics capacity
Scenario input: sales growth %, hiring, capacity expansion
Demand shock → required units
Convert units → required resources (labour hrs, inventory units, machine hrs, shipments)
Utilisation = required ÷ capacity for each resource
Any utilisation > 100% ?
YES → that is the bottleneck; compute max feasible sales & shortfall
Suggest cheapest fix (hire, shift, buy stock, add carrier)
Feasibility verdict, bottleneck, cost of closing the gap

Pseudocode

required_units = base_units * (1 + growth)
need = {labour: required_units * hrs_per_unit,
        stock:  required_units * (1 + safety),
        machine: required_units * mach_hrs_per_unit,
        logistics: required_units / units_per_shipment}
util = {r: need[r] / capacity[r] for r in need}
bottleneck = argmax(util)
max_growth = min(capacity[r] / need_at_base[r] for r in need) - 1
if util[bottleneck] > 1: fix = cheapest_fix(bottleneck, need - capacity)
